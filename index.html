Financial Terms Glossary

A portion of a company's profit paid out to shareholders, usually quarterly, in proportion to the number of shares held.

An instruction to buy or sell immediately at the best available current price. Execution is fast but the exact price is not guaranteed.

An instruction to buy or sell only at a specified price or better. It gives price control but may never execute.

The complete collection of investments held by an individual, including stocks, cash and other assets.

The difference between the money made and the money lost on an investment over a given period.

A statistical measure of how much a price fluctuates over time. Higher volatility means larger and faster price swings.

The total market value of a company's outstanding shares, calculated as share price multiplied by shares outstanding.

A sustained period of rising prices and optimistic sentiment across a market or asset class.

A prolonged period of falling prices, typically defined as a decline of 20% or more from recent highs.

Spreading investments across sectors, geographies and asset types to reduce the impact of any single loss.

A chart symbol showing the open, high, low and close price for a specific time period in a single visual.

An initial public offering — the first time a private company sells shares to the general public on an exchange.

Return on investment, expressed as a percentage of the amount originally invested.

India's two primary stock exchanges — the National Stock Exchange and the Bombay Stock Exchange.

The Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator that oversees Indian securities markets and investor protection.
